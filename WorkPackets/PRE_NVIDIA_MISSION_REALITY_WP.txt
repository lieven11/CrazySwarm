PURPOSE_BEGIN
Close the gap between a deterministic Fast Sim demonstration and the stated goal
that one immutable Python mission artifact can execute, without source changes,
through Fast Sim, a mocked Isaac gateway, a later live Isaac backend, and a real
Crazyflie adapter.

The work is deliberately split into software-only, bench-hardware, contained-flight,
and external-reference gates. A Crazyflie 2.1+ with Flow Deck V2 and Multi-ranger
Deck is assumed available for the hardware packets. Those decks are useful onboard
sensors, but they are not independent ground truth. They cannot by themselves prove
digital-twin trajectory accuracy.

This file is written before implementation. It defines the new gate, mission corpus,
tests, evidence, and reality limits. Mission files, adapters, and test code are created
only when the applicable packet is executed.
PURPOSE_END

DECISION_SUMMARY_BEGIN
- Retain Fast Sim. Do not rewrite it into a photorealistic or hardware-claimed simulator.
- Do not accept the current nominal hover as proof of physical hover fidelity.
- Repair mission-level adapter conformance, online health enforcement, estimator/control
  boundaries, and backend-neutral runtime selection before detailed Isaac physics work.
- Permit NVIDIA-ISAAC-WP-00 architecture work after REALITY-WP-00.
- Treat the mock portion of NVIDIA-ISAAC-WP-02 as part of REALITY-WP-02.
- Permit primitive USD/controller scaffolding after the software gate, but hold
  NVIDIA-ISAAC-WP-03/WP-04 qualification, WP-05 closure, and default-backend promotion
  until the physical data package required by the selected model profile exists.
- Keep DIGITAL_TWIN disabled until a real adapter, synchronized measured telemetry,
  and an independent external reference have passed their own qualification.
DECISION_SUMMARY_END

ASSUMED_HARDWARE_PROFILE_BEGIN
AIRCRAFT:
- One Crazyflie 2.1+ in stock or explicitly recorded configuration.
- Exact serial/identity, firmware version, controller, estimator, propeller, motor,
  battery, and deck revisions must be recorded; no value may be inferred from a product page.

DECKS:
- Flow Deck V2 installed below the vehicle.
- Multi-ranger Deck installed above the vehicle.
- Official Bitcraze compatibility documentation lists this deck combination as compatible
  on Crazyflie 2.1(+), subject to correct mounting and standard firmware assumptions.
- Flow Deck V2 provides optical-flow and downward time-of-flight measurements that are
  fused by the Crazyflie estimator.
- Multi-ranger provides front, back, left, right, and up time-of-flight ranges.
- The combined canonical range set is front/back/left/right/up from Multi-ranger and
  down/zrange from Flow Deck V2.

REQUIRED_SUPPORT_EQUIPMENT_BEFORE_HARDWARE_EXECUTION:
- Compatible Crazyradio and USB host access.
- Known-good charger and at least two identifiable batteries.
- Prop guards, marked exclusion zone, fire-safe charging/storage practice, and an
  operator-controlled emergency procedure.
- A contained flight area or net for automated trials.
- Surveyed floor, obstacles, lighting, and surface descriptions.
- A calibrated scale for exact takeoff mass.
- An independent external position reference for any claim about true drift,
  trajectory error, simulator residual, or digital-twin accuracy.

EXTERNAL_REFERENCE_POLICY:
- Flow-derived stateEstimate is onboard estimated state, not independent truth.
- Multi-ranger ranges are local obstacle measurements, not global pose.
- An overhead calibrated camera/marker tracker, Lighthouse configured as an independent
  reference, Loco/UWB reference, or motion capture may satisfy the external-reference
  requirement only after its frame, latency, rate, uncertainty, and independence are recorded.
- Without an external reference, hardware packets may establish functional operation,
  sensor presence, timing, command semantics, and onboard repeatability only.
- Without an external reference, PHYSICAL_TRAJECTORY_QUALIFIED and DIGITAL_TWIN remain false.
ASSUMED_HARDWARE_PROFILE_END

HARDWARE_REALITY_LIMITS_BEGIN
- Flow-only horizontal position is relative to startup and derived by integrating motion;
  it is not a persistent surveyed global coordinate.
- Flow performance depends on height, floor texture, reflectivity, lighting, motion,
  estimator configuration, deck alignment, vibration, and takeoff behavior near the floor.
- The downward range represents distance to the surface below. Flying over an object or
  height discontinuity can alter the observed ground distance and must be tested explicitly.
- Multi-ranger detects the nearest surface in five directions, with maximum product-range
  claims up to 4 m under suitable conditions. Material, angle, lighting, no-return behavior,
  clipping, and cross-talk must be measured for the actual room.
- Multi-ranger does not classify objects, build a map, or implement avoidance by itself.
- The real Crazyflie firmware estimator and controller stabilize the vehicle. Ground-station
  Python supplies mission intent and supervision; it must not be treated as the motor loop.
- High-level position trajectories and streamed hover/velocity setpoints have different
  communication and watchdog behavior. The real adapter command mapping must be selected
  by experiment for the Flow-only profile rather than assumed.
- Relative mission completion can be tested with Flow, but precise global return, docking,
  tight formation, and cross-vehicle separation eventually require a qualified shared
  global reference.
HARDWARE_REALITY_LIMITS_END

MISSION_EXECUTION_INVARIANT_BEGIN
For every supported backend, the following must remain identical:
1. Original Python source bytes and SHA-256.
2. Mission runtime ID/version and allowed-language version.
3. Validated mission parameters and canonical frames/units.
4. Approved command intent kind, values, ordering, ownership, and safety policy.
5. Mission cancellation, timeout, abort, emergency, failure, and evidence semantics.
6. Unsupported-data behavior: absent remains absent and is never converted to zero.

The following are expected to differ and are compared with declared tolerances:
- telemetry sample timestamps and rates;
- estimated trajectory and physical trajectory;
- controller setpoints and motor outputs below the canonical adapter boundary;
- battery/current response;
- sensor noise, dropout, latency, and range behavior;
- contact behavior and environmental disturbances.

`SAME PYTHON MISSION` means the same immutable source artifact produces the same
canonical mission intent through the same supervisor. It does not mean byte-identical
physics or that simulation values are copied from real telemetry.
MISSION_EXECUTION_INVARIANT_END

MISSION_RUNTIME_TIERS_BEGIN
TIER_A_LINEAR_OPERATOR_ARTIFACT:
- Preserves the existing restricted literal `await drone.<command>(...)` format.
- Suitable for takeoff, hover, relative move, yaw, and land sequences.
- Must remain supported without source changes.

TIER_B_BOUNDED_OBSERVATION_MISSION:
- Required for Multi-ranger response, Flow-quality response, and later fleet logic.
- Repository-owned and versioned initially; operator upload may be enabled only after
  the same isolation and review gate passes.
- May use typed assignment, arithmetic, comparison, `if`, bounded `for`, deadline-bound
  `while`, and `await drone.observe(...)` through a minimal canonical API.
- May not import modules, access files/network/processes, use reflection/dynamic attributes,
  call adapters directly, create unmanaged tasks, or disable supervisor checks.
- Every loop requires a static maximum iteration count or explicit monotonic deadline.
- Online execution uses a sandboxed request/response protocol. It must not precompute an
  observation-dependent mission into a static command list.
- Mission safety remains in the supervisor; a mission may add recovery intent but may
  never weaken health, geofence, speed, altitude, or emergency policy.
MISSION_RUNTIME_TIERS_END

CANONICAL_MISSION_CORPUS_BEGIN
All executable qualification mission files live under `missions/qualification/` when implemented.
All source hashes are frozen only after review and the complete software gate passes.

QF-01 `hover_30s.py`
- Tier: A
- Source basis: exact current `missions/hover30s.py` bytes.
- Sequence: takeoff 0.30 m / 2.0 s; hover 30.0 s; land / 2.0 s.
- Purpose: nominal timing, altitude hold, telemetry continuity, battery trend,
  intervention, evidence, replay, and cross-backend source identity.

QF-02 `move_return.py`
- Tier: A
- Sequence: takeoff 0.30 m; hover 1 s; move +0.30 m HOME X / 2 s;
  hover 1 s; move -0.30 m HOME X / 2 s; hover 1 s; land.
- Purpose: relative translation, return error, Flow estimator drift, frame semantics,
  path evidence, and no overlapping command behavior.

QF-03 `square_relative.py`
- Tier: A
- Sequence: takeoff; four explicit 0.25 m HOME-frame legs with 0.5 s hover between
  legs; land. No loop is used in the source so Tier A remains sufficient.
- Purpose: accumulated translation error, corner overshoot, estimator repeatability,
  and final return residual.

QF-04 `body_yaw.py`
- Tier: A
- Sequence: takeoff; BODY-frame +0.20 m move; +90 degree yaw over 3 s; BODY-frame
  +0.20 m move; hover; land.
- Purpose: body/home transform signs, yaw convention, Flow behavior during yaw,
  quaternion/Euler logging, and simulator/firmware frame parity.

QF-05 `range_approach_stop.py`
- Tier: B
- Sequence: takeoff; obtain bounded observations; approach a surveyed flat obstacle
  in small completed increments at no more than 0.10 m/s; stop when valid front range
  is at or below 0.60 m; hover 2 s; retreat 0.20 m; land.
- Purpose: online Python decision execution, range freshness/validity, obstacle margin,
  no-hit behavior, deadline enforcement, and the same-source real/sim requirement.
- Fail closed: unavailable/stale/invalid front range requests controlled hover then
  abort-and-land through supervisor policy; it never means infinite clearance.

QF-06 `range_direction_escape.py`
- Tier: B
- Sequence: takeoff at a surveyed center point; observe front/back/left/right/up;
  select one bounded retreat direction only when exactly one valid minimum is below
  the configured threshold; hover and land.
- Purpose: direction mapping, identity of range signals, tie handling, missing data,
  and deterministic branching.
- This is a sensor-reaction test, not a general obstacle-avoidance claim.

QF-07 `flow_out_back.py`
- Tier: B
- Sequence: takeoff; capture valid initial estimate; move +0.30 m and -0.30 m;
  compare source-compatible estimate to the initial estimate; land.
- Purpose: online observation API, estimator validity, drift visibility, and explicit
  separation of onboard estimate from external reference.
- Mission success is based on safe command completion. Accuracy qualification is
  computed by the evaluator, not self-certified by the mission.

QF-08 `observation_timeout.py`
- Tier: B; software/mock only unless a reviewed fault-injection method exists.
- Sequence: takeoff in simulator; request an observation with a bounded timeout while
  the adapter withholds required position/range data.
- Expected result: ABORTED or FAILED with a named observation/health reason, controlled
  recovery, no continued move command, and immutable evidence.

QF-09 `hover_cancel.py`
- Tier: A; source may be identical to QF-01.
- Execution overlay: cancel at 5 s into hover.
- Expected result: controlled abort-and-land, ABORTED/MISSION_CANCELLED, no emergency event.

QF-10 `hover_emergency.py`
- Tier: A; source may be identical to QF-01.
- Execution overlay: emergency command during hover.
- Expected result in software: immediate motor-stop semantics and distinct evidence.
- Real-aircraft motor-cut testing is props-off by default. Any airborne execution requires
  a separately approved capture apparatus and procedure; it is not mandatory to close
  the functional real-adapter gate.

QF-11 `floor_height_transition.py`
- Tier: B; simulator mandatory, physical execution separately reviewed.
- Sequence: takeoff over the baseline floor; approach a wide, low, surveyed platform
  while monitoring external altitude, onboard estimated altitude, and down/zrange;
  stop before crossing unless the physical procedure explicitly authorizes the transition.
- Purpose: expose the difference between height above the current surface and global
  altitude, prevent an undetected altitude jump over objects, and qualify simulator
  Flow/down-range semantics.
- Physical crossing is not required for the initial functional gate. If executed, the
  platform must be broad, low, soft-edged/contained, and covered by an independent reference.

NEGATIVE_ARTIFACTS:
- `reject_import.py`: import or host access; rejected before connection.
- `reject_unbounded_loop.py`: loop with no static limit/deadline; rejected before connection.
- `reject_direct_adapter.py`: adapter/cflib access; rejected before connection.
- `reject_missing_land.py`: incomplete flight lifecycle; rejected before connection.
- `reject_unknown_frame.py`: invalid frame; rejected before connection.
- `reject_dynamic_code.py`: eval/exec/reflection/dynamic attribute; rejected before connection.
- `reject_excessive_commands.py`: static/runtime command budget exceeded; rejected or
  safely aborted with no authority leak.
CANONICAL_MISSION_CORPUS_END

COMMON_TEST_THRESHOLDS_BEGIN
All thresholds below are initial engineering acceptance limits, not manufacturer
specifications. Hardware thresholds may be tightened after baseline evidence. They
may not be relaxed after a failed run without a written root-cause and review record.

IDENTITY_AND_INTENT:
- Source bytes and SHA-256: exact equality across backends.
- Canonical command-intent trace: exact semantic equality after removing backend-local
  timestamps, process IDs, and transport IDs.
- No duplicate takeoff, move, land, abort, or emergency intent after reconnect/retry.
- Every command and sample belongs to the correct vehicle, run, source clock, and model.

REALTIME:
- QF-01 requested flight sequence is 34.0 s; expected source duration includes only
  declared bounded connection/acknowledgement overhead.
- Realtime backend wall/source duration ratio: 0.98 through 1.02 for Fast Sim and
  mock timing tests; physical adapter records actual ratio and requires 0.95 through
  1.05 unless the command contract explicitly acknowledges asynchronously.
- No nominal telemetry gap greater than 0.50 s.

NOMINAL HOVER SOFTWARE:
- Takeoff completion truth altitude error: <= 0.03 m.
- Thirty-second hover truth RMS altitude error from 0.30 m: <= 0.02 m.
- Thirty-second hover truth peak altitude error: <= 0.05 m.
- Horizontal truth displacement during a no-disturbance run: <= 0.05 m.
- No non-finite state, quaternion norm error > 1e-9, unexplained energy increase,
  or motor saturation lasting more than 0.10 s.
- Final altitude <= 0.01 m; armed false; flying false; motor command/thrust/current zero.

NOMINAL HOVER PHYSICAL_WITH_EXTERNAL_REFERENCE:
- Safety containment radius from takeoff point: 0.50 m maximum.
- Mean altitude error: <= 0.05 m; peak altitude error: <= 0.10 m.
- Horizontal displacement after 30 s: <= 0.20 m for the initial Flow-only gate.
- Roll/pitch remain within the reviewed flight envelope; any excursion over 20 degrees
  or containment-boundary approach is an automatic aborted trial.
- Landing completes without tip-over, prop strike, uncontrolled bounce, or motor run-on.
- These limits establish a conservative first baseline, not precise-position qualification.

MOVE_AND_RETURN_WITH_EXTERNAL_REFERENCE:
- No containment or obstacle-margin violation.
- Each 0.30 m commanded leg completes in the correct signed direction.
- Final external-reference horizontal error <= 0.15 m for QF-02.
- QF-03 final external-reference horizontal error <= 0.25 m for the first Flow-only
  square baseline; the onboard-estimate error is reported separately.
- Without external reference, these accuracy fields are UNAVAILABLE, not PASS.

RANGE_APPROACH:
- Surveyed flat obstacle; commanded approach speed <= 0.10 m/s.
- Stop threshold 0.60 m; minimum permitted external measured clearance 0.40 m.
- Range age at the decision <= 0.20 s.
- Invalid, stale, clipped, or unavailable range may not be interpreted as clear space.
- Ten consecutive trials per selected surface/lighting condition with no clearance violation.

TELEMETRY_MINIMUMS:
- Estimated pose/velocity stream target >= 25 Hz, with achieved rate and loss recorded.
- Attitude/angular-rate target >= 50 Hz when transport budget permits; otherwise a
  measured lower supported rate is declared before twin comparison.
- Front/back/left/right/up/down ranges >= 10 Hz.
- Battery and radio/link observations >= 5 Hz.
- External reference >= 30 Hz preferred and never below 20 Hz for trajectory metrics.
- Rates are receive-side measured rates; configuration alone is not evidence.

REPETITIONS:
- Software deterministic nominal missions: two clean-process identical outcomes.
- Software seeded disturbance/fault cases: at least 100 seeds per class.
- Hardware static sensor points: at least 30 samples per point/condition.
- Hardware QF-01 nominal hover: three successful short-hover trials followed by ten
  30-second trials across at least two batteries.
- Hardware move/range trials: ten trials per accepted configuration.
COMMON_TEST_THRESHOLDS_END
